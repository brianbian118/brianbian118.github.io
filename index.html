I am a PhD student at the University of British Columbia, advised by Prof. Kenneth Chau. My research explores how artificial intelligence can understand human behavior and intention through multimodal perception, contextual reasoning, and long-horizon interaction.

My broader goal is to build AI systems that understand people naturally, rather than requiring people to learn how to interact with machines. I also work on computer vision, material appearance understanding, and physically based rendering.

我目前在英属哥伦比亚大学（UBC Okanagan）攻读电气工程博士学位，导师为 Kenneth Chau 教授。我的研究关注人工智能如何通过多模态感知、情境推理和长时交互，理解人类行为与意图。

我的长期目标是探索能够自然理解人的 AI 系统，而不是要求人适应机器的交互方式。我也从事计算机视觉、材质外观理解和基于物理的渲染相关研究。

Research研究方向

Central question. How can AI reliably infer human intentions from observable evidence, represent context over time, and know when the evidence is insufficient?

核心研究问题。 AI 如何根据可观察证据可靠地理解人的意图、持续表征环境与情境，并识别证据尚不足以支持判断的情况？

01 / Human-centered AI以人为中心的 AI

Nonverbal Intention Understanding非语言意图理解

Studying how multimodal systems interpret gesture, action, social behavior, and contextual cues to reason about what people intend to do.

研究多模态系统如何结合手势、动作、社会行为与情境线索，推断人类行为背后的意图。

02 / Multimodal intelligence多模态智能

Context, Memory & Long-Horizon Interaction情境、记忆与长时交互

Exploring persistent representations of people, events, and environments, with particular attention to temporal consistency and evidence-grounded reasoning.

探索人物、事件与环境的持续表征，重点关注时间一致性以及有证据支持的推理。

03 / Computer vision计算机视觉

Visual Material Understanding视觉材质理解

Investigating image-based material retrieval, appearance representations, and physically meaningful evaluation of visual similarity.

研究基于图像的材质检索、外观表征，以及具有物理意义的视觉相似性评估。

Ongoing doctoral research博士研究 · 进行中

Multimodal Reasoning for Human Intention面向人类意图的多模态推理

Research on integrating visual observations and contextual evidence for more reliable interpretation of human behavior, including uncertainty and longer-term context.

研究如何整合视觉观察与情境证据，更可靠地理解人类行为，同时考虑判断的不确定性与长期情境信息。

Applied computer vision应用计算机视觉

Appearance-Based Material Retrieval基于外观的材质检索

Developing and evaluating methods for retrieving material identity from visual observations, while studying the distinction between learned visual similarity and physical appearance.

开发并评估从视觉观察中检索材质身份的方法，研究学习到的视觉相似性与真实物理外观之间的差异。

Interactive AI prototype交互式 AI 原型

Gesture-Aware Visual Assistant手势感知视觉助手

A prototype connecting hand-motion perception to context-aware interaction for accessible, natural control of everyday environments.

通过手部动作感知与情境交互相结合，探索对日常环境更自然、更易使用的控制方式。

Rendering & optics渲染与光学

Physically Based Fabric Appearance基于物理的织物外观建模

Investigating the relationship between light transport, measured material response, and photorealistic rendering of textiles.

探索光传输、实测材质响应以及织物真实感渲染之间的关系。
